# How long do you keep your records?

You keep your business records for 7 years. Here you read what that covers
and in which form you keep it.

## Seven years

You are required to keep your records for 7 years. The basic records you
always keep for 7 years: your sales and purchase records, your debtors and
creditors, and the general ledger. For records about real estate another
period applies. It is on the source page below.

## What you keep

Your records are everything you lay down about your business, on paper or
digitally. What a bookkeeper keeps for you is part of it. The duty to keep
your records stays when you stop your business.

## In which form

You keep your records in the form you have them in: digitally or on paper.
If you keep them digitally, you make sure the Dutch Tax Administration
(Belastingdienst) can use your programs and files during an audit. Keeping
only a printout of your files is not enough.

## If you use the small business scheme

With the small business scheme (KOR) you keep your purchase invoices too.

## How to do this in Huismus

Your records are on your own computer or your own server, also when you move
on to something else later. An issued invoice is fixed and stays the way you
sent it. Huismus makes a backup every day in every place you turn on: a
folder, Dropbox or WebDAV.
