# VAT return: when, and what do you fill in?

If you charge VAT on your invoices, you file a VAT return: usually every
quarter. Here you read when the return is due and which amounts go in it.

If you use the small business scheme (KOR), you no longer file VAT returns.
Read [The small business scheme (KOR)](kor.en.md) instead.

## How often

You file a VAT return per month, per quarter or per year. Most businesses
file per quarter. The Dutch Tax Administration (Belastingdienst) sends you a
letter that says which period is yours.

## The deadline

Per quarter or per month you file no later than the last day of the month
after that quarter or month. Per quarter that is:

| Quarter | File by |
|---|---|
| January to March | 30 April |
| April to June | 31 July |
| July to September | 31 October |
| October to December | 31 January |

If you file per year, it is before 1 April of the year after.

## Also without VAT in a period

You always file a return, also when you charged no VAT in a period.

## What you fill in

The return holds the VAT you charged your customers and the VAT you can
deduct. The example of the Belastingdienst names these boxes:

- **1a, supplies and services taxed at the high rate:** your revenue without
  VAT and the VAT on it.
- **5a, VAT due:** the total of boxes 1 to 4.
- **5b, input VAT:** the VAT on your purchases that you can deduct.

You pay the difference between 5a and 5b, or you get it back.

## How to do this in Huismus

Under **Reports → VAT return** you see the revenue and the VAT from your
invoices and expenses per quarter, month or year, per box, and what you pay
or get back. You copy the amounts into your return yourself. Huismus sends
nothing to the tax office.
