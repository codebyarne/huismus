# What goes on an invoice?

A Dutch invoice carries a fixed list of details: who you are, who your
customer is, what you supplied and the amount and VAT that go with it. Here
you read which details those are and when you send the invoice at the latest.

## The details on every invoice

The Dutch Tax Administration (Belastingdienst) asks for these details on
every invoice:

- your full name and your customer's;
- your full address and your customer's;
- your VAT identification number, the one that starts with NL;
- your KVK number, if you are registered with the Chamber of Commerce (KVK);
- the date you issue the invoice;
- an invoice number;
- what you supplied. For goods: which and how many. For services: which and
  their extent;
- the date you supplied them, or the date of an advance payment;
- the amount you charge, without VAT;
- the VAT rate;
- the VAT amount.

## The invoice number

Your invoices have consecutive numbers, in one or more series. Each number
occurs only once.

## When you send the invoice

You send the invoice no later than the 15th day of the month after the month
in which you supplied. If you supply on 10 March, that is 15 April at the
latest.

## An amount up to € 100

If the invoice amount is € 100 or less including VAT, fewer requirements
apply. The Belastingdienst calls this a simplified invoice. The source page
below says which details it carries.

## If you use the small business scheme

With the small business scheme (KOR) you put no VAT on your invoices. For VAT
you don't even have to send invoices. If you do send them, you state on them
that an exemption applies. Read more about the scheme in
[The small business scheme (KOR)](kor.en.md).

## How to do this in Huismus

Huismus has a place on the invoice for each of these details. You fill in
your name, address, KVK number and VAT number once under **Settings →
Business**. The delivery date you fill in per invoice. An
invoice gets its number when you issue it: the next one in the series,
without gaps. Huismus uses the same full layout for every amount, also up to
€ 100.
