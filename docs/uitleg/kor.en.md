# The small business scheme (KOR)

With the small business scheme (KOR) you charge your customers no VAT and you
no longer file VAT returns. In exchange, you don't deduct the VAT on your
costs. Here you read when you can take part, what it means and how you sign
up.

## When you can take part

You can take part if:

- your business is established in the Netherlands;
- your revenue per calendar year is € 20,000 at most. That holds for the year
  in which you sign up and for the year before it;
- what you supply is subject to VAT.

## What taking part means

- You charge your customers no VAT, so you pay none to the tax office either.
- You put no VAT on your invoices. For VAT you don't even have to send
  invoices. If you do send them, you state on them that an exemption applies.
- You no longer file VAT returns. In a few cases you still file a one-off
  return.
- You don't deduct the VAT on your business costs and investments.
- You do keep your purchase invoices.

## Signing up

You sign up in Mijn Belastingdienst Zakelijk, the tax office's portal for
businesses. You can do so once you are registered for VAT. Your registration
has to arrive at least 4 weeks before the start date. If you file per
quarter, these are the dates:

| Taking part from | Sign up by |
|---|---|
| 1 January | 3 December |
| 1 April | 3 March |
| 1 July | 2 June |
| 1 October | 2 September |

Within 8 weeks you get a letter with the final start date. Until that date
you keep filing VAT returns.

## Stopping

You can leave the scheme at any time, at least 4 weeks before you want it to
end. After that you can't take part for the rest of that calendar year and
the year after. If you go over € 20,000, you leave at once. Read about that
in [The revenue limit of the KOR](kor-grens.en.md).

## How to do this in Huismus

Under **Settings → VAT** you choose **No, I use the KOR**. Every line on your
invoices then has 0% VAT. You write the text that goes under your invoices
yourself: Huismus doesn't suggest one.
