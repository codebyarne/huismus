# The revenue limit of the small business scheme (KOR)

With the small business scheme (KOR) your revenue in a calendar year may not
go over € 20,000. Here you read what counts and what you do when you go over
it.

## What counts

The limit holds per calendar year. If you use the KOR, your revenue is the
total of all amounts you charge your customers.

## When you go over it

As soon as your revenue in a calendar year goes over € 20,000, you may no
longer use the KOR. You deregister at that moment, right away.

The supply or service that takes you over the limit already falls outside
the exemption. You charge VAT on its whole amount. If you are at € 19,500
and you supply a service of € 1,000, you charge VAT on the whole € 1,000.

## Leaving the scheme

You leave in Mijn Belastingdienst Zakelijk, the tax office's portal for
businesses. Within 8 weeks you get a letter with the final end date.

## After that

After you leave, you can't take part for the rest of that calendar year and
the year after. Then you can sign up again. The conditions are in
[The small business scheme (KOR)](kor.en.md).

## How to do this in Huismus

Under **Settings → VAT** you fill in the **KOR limit** you want to watch. The
overview shows how much of it you have used this year. From the percentage
you choose, you get a notice. If a new invoice takes you over that
percentage, Huismus says so when you issue it.
