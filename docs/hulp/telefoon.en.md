# On your phone

Does Huismus run on a server, such as a Raspberry Pi or a NAS? Then you can
put it on your phone, tablet or laptop as an app. You don't need an app
store for it. You get its own icon and a window without an address bar.

## Installing Huismus as an app

Open Huismus in the browser and choose:

- **iPhone and iPad:** Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** Chrome → menu → **Install app**.
- **Chrome or Edge on a computer:** the install icon in the address bar, or
  menu → **Install app**.
- **Mac:** Safari → File → **Add to Dock**.

This only works when the address of Huismus starts with `https://`, or when
you open it on the computer it runs on (through `localhost`). Don't see the
option? Check the start of the address first.

## Sharing a PDF

The **PDF** button on an invoice, credit note or quote opens a page with the
PDF. At the top there's a button back to the document. In the app on your
home screen there's **Share**: use it to mail the PDF or save it to
Files. In the browser there's **Download**, and **Share** too if the browser
can share files.

## What's on your phone

Your records aren't on it. Everything stays on the server, and the app only
shows what the server sends. If the app can't reach the server, for example
because you have no wifi, your VPN is off or the server is off, a Huismus
page tells you so, instead of a browser error.
