# Hours

With Hours you keep track of how many hours you work for a customer, and at
what rate. Switch Hours on at Settings › Menu.

## Entering hours

At the top of **Hours**, fill in the date, the customer, a description and
the number of hours, for example 1.5 or 1:30. Click **Add**. On your phone
you can also use **+** and then **Hours**.

The rate is per hour. Huismus fills in the rate of your last hours for that
customer. If you charge VAT, the rate is excluding VAT.

Hours you don't bill, such as a meeting? Leave the rate empty. The list
then says **No rate**.

Huismus keeps 1:20 as 1.33 hours. That way the list shows what you'll bill
later too.

## The list

Your hours are listed per week, newest first. Each week shows how many hours
it was and what they're worth. The list starts at this month. Choose another
month or a customer to see only those. Above the list is the total of what
you see.

## An invoice from your hours

You turn your hours into an invoice: choose **Create invoice…** in
**Hours**, then the customer and the month. Huismus puts the hours with a
rate on a draft invoice, which you check and then issue.

Choose whether the invoice gets one line per description or one line per
day. Huismus adds up the hours of such a line. That can make the total a
cent different from Hours; the page then says so.

Hours on an invoice can't be changed or deleted any more. The list shows
which invoice they're on. If you delete the draft invoice, the hours become
free again. With a credit note they stay on the invoice.

## Changing or deleting

Click a row to change it. You can also delete it there.

You can archive a customer who has hours. Those hours stay in the list. New
hours only go on customers who aren't archived.

## Switching and exporting

Huismus doesn't bring your hours over from Moneybird yet.

When you export at **Settings › Export**, the zip holds **hours.csv** if
you have hours in that period. See [Exporting](exporteren.en.md).
