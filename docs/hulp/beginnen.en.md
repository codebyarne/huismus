# Getting started

The first time you open Huismus, your records are empty. There are three ways
to start: step by step, by importing from Moneybird, or by looking around with
sample data first.

## Start step by step

On the overview, click **Start step by step**. In four steps you fill in what
Huismus needs for your first invoice:

1. **Business:** your business name, address, KvK number and IBAN. This goes
   on every invoice and quote.
2. **KOR:** whether you use the small business scheme (KOR), and if so the
   text that goes under your invoices. More in [KOR and VAT](kor-en-btw.en.md).
3. **Invoices:** your default payment term and how Huismus numbers your
   invoices and quotes. The default is usually fine.
4. **Logo:** your logo goes at the top of your invoices and quotes. This can
   wait.

You can skip a step and come back later. Everything is also under
**Settings** afterwards.

## Moving from Moneybird

Coming from Moneybird? Click **Import from Moneybird** and bring all your
customers, invoices, quotes and expenses along in one go. Huismus does a trial
run first and only saves anything when you choose to. How it works is in
[Moving from Moneybird](moneybird.en.md). Only cancel Moneybird once
everything is in Huismus: see [Switching to Huismus](overstappen.en.md).

## Look around first

Want to see how Huismus works first? Choose **Try with sample data**. Huismus
then fills itself with nine years of made-up records: customers, invoices,
quotes and expenses. Click anything you like: none of it is real.

Done looking? **Clear sample data** takes Huismus back to how it was. Anything
you made or set up in the meantime is gone too. While the sample data is in,
Huismus makes no backups.

## Ready to invoice

At first, the overview has a short list of what's left before your first
invoice: your business details, your logo, the KOR or VAT, your first customer
and your first invoice. What's done gets a tick. **Start** takes you straight
to the right place.

## After that

- **Backups:** choose a folder for your backups, for example in iCloud Drive
  or OneDrive. See [Backups](back-ups.en.md).
- **Mail:** to mail invoices from Huismus, set up your mail. See
  [Mail](mailen.en.md).
- **Your first invoice:** see [Invoices](facturen.en.md).
