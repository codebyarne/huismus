# Getting started

The first time you open Huismus, your records are empty. There are three ways
to start: step by step, by importing from Moneybird, or by looking around with
sample data first.

## Start step by step

On the overview, click **Start step by step**. Step by step you fill in what
Huismus needs for your first invoice:

1. **Business:** your business name, address, KvK number and IBAN. This goes
   on every invoice and quote.
2. **Your work:** three questions. Do you charge VAT, do you use the
   small business scheme (KOR), do you charge no VAT for another reason, or
   don't you know yet? Do you have customers where the VAT is reverse-charged?
   And in which language do you usually write your invoices? Your answers
   decide which steps follow. More about the KOR in
   [Small business scheme (KOR) and VAT](kor-en-btw.en.md).
3. **Small business scheme (KOR):** only if you use the KOR. The text that
   goes under your invoices, and your KOR limit.
4. **No VAT:** only if you charge no VAT for another reason. The text that
   goes under your invoices. Huismus doesn't fill it in for you.
5. **Reverse charge:** only if you have customers where the VAT is
   reverse-charged. The text under an invoice with a "reverse charge" line.
   Huismus doesn't fill it in for you; you can leave it empty.
6. **Invoices:** your default payment term and how Huismus numbers your
   invoices and quotes. The default is usually fine. Coming from another
   package? Enter the sequence number that follows your last invoice, so
   your numbers carry on.
7. **Logo:** your logo goes at the top of your invoices and quotes. This can
   wait.

You can skip a step and come back later. Everything is also under
**Settings** afterwards: the KOR, the no-VAT text and the reverse-charge
text under **VAT**, your invoice language under
**Invoices and numbering**. Go through the steps again and Your work shows
what is set now.

## Moving from Moneybird

Coming from Moneybird? Click **Import from Moneybird** and bring all your
customers, invoices, quotes and expenses along in one go. Huismus does a trial
run first and only saves anything when you choose to. How it works is in
[Moving from Moneybird](moneybird.en.md).

## Look around first

Want to see how Huismus works first? Choose **Try with sample data**. Huismus
then fills itself with nine years of made-up records: customers, invoices,
quotes and expenses. Click anything you like: none of it is real.

Done looking? **Clear sample data** takes Huismus back to how it was. Anything
you made or set up in the meantime is gone too. While the sample data is in,
Huismus makes no backups.

## Ready to invoice

At first, the overview has a short list of what's left before your first
invoice: your business details, your logo, the KOR or VAT, your first customer
and your first invoice. What's done gets a tick. **Start** takes you straight
to the right place.

## After that

- **Backups:** choose a folder for your backups, for example in iCloud Drive
  or OneDrive. See [Backups](back-ups.en.md).
- **Mail:** to mail invoices from Huismus, set up your mail. See
  [Mail](mailen.en.md).
- **Your first invoice:** see [Invoices](facturen.en.md).
