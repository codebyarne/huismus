# Quotes

You make a quote just like an invoice. You send it, keep track of the
customer's answer, and turn it into an invoice with one click.

## Making and sending a quote

Under **Quotes**, click **New quote**. Choose a customer and fill in the
lines, as with an invoice. Under **Valid until**, fill in how long your offer
stands.

Ready? Click **Send…**. The quote then gets a number. After that you download
the PDF or mail it to the customer. See [Mail](mailen.en.md).

You can still change a sent quote. Huismus does tell you to send the new PDF
again if the customer already has it.

## The customer's answer

At the top of the quote you see which step it's in. Got an answer? Record it
with **Accept** or **Decline**. No answer and the quote has run out? Choose
**Mark as expired**.

On the overview, **Awaiting reply** lists the quotes that are still open.

## From quote to invoice

Click **Convert to invoice**. Huismus makes a draft invoice with the quote's
customer and lines. The invoice stays linked to the quote, and the quote
itself doesn't change. Then you issue the invoice as usual. See
[Invoices](facturen.en.md).

You can also convert a quote that expired or was declined.
