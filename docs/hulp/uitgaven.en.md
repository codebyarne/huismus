# Expenses

Keep track of your costs, with the receipt attached. The year overview then
shows your revenue, your costs and what's left.

## Adding an expense

Under **Expenses**, click **New expense**, or **Expense** on the overview.
Fill in the date, the supplier and a description, and for each line the
amount and a category.

Fill in the gross amount: what you actually paid, including any VAT. If you
use the KOR, the VAT is optional. If you want to see it, fill it in as well.
It's for information only and isn't counted anywhere.

Don't use the KOR? Then fill in the VAT on every line as on the receipt, and
choose the VAT rate: 21%, 9%, 0% or no VAT, or reverse charge (Netherlands,
another EU country or outside the EU). No VAT on the receipt? Fill in 0 and
choose 0% or no VAT. Huismus doesn't compute the VAT: it keeps what you fill
in.

Did you pay for things from different categories at once, like a laptop and a
bag? Use **Add line** and give each line its own category.

## Receipts

Drop a receipt on the expense, or choose a file: a photo (JPG, PNG or HEIC)
or a PDF. Huismus keeps a HEIC photo as a JPG. For a new expense you can do this at the top of the form, before you click
**Save**. An expense can have more than one receipt: add the others after
saving.

Receipts from the road can also go in a folder that Huismus picks up
itself. See [Receipts on the road](bonnetjes.en.md).

## Categories

Under **Categories** on the Expenses page you add, rename and order your
categories. The order decides how they appear in drop-down lists and on the
year overview.

## Searching and the year overview

At the top of the list you search by supplier or description, and filter by
year and category.

**Reports → Year overview** shows your revenue, your costs and the difference
for each year, also per quarter. If you don't use the KOR, those are your
costs excluding VAT, with the input VAT apart (see [KOR and VAT](kor-en-btw.en.md)).
You can download a CSV there too, or an export with everything in it. See [Exporting](exporteren.en.md).
