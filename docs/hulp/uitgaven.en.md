# Expenses

Keep track of your costs, with the receipt attached. The year overview then
shows your revenue, your costs and what's left.

## Adding an expense

Under **Expenses**, click **New expense**, or **Expense** on the overview.
Fill in the date, the supplier and a description, and for each line the
amount and a category.

Fill in the gross amount: what you actually paid, including any VAT. If you
want to see the VAT too, fill that in as well. It's for information only and
isn't counted anywhere.

Did you pay for things from different categories at once, like a laptop and a
bag? Use **Add line** and give each line its own category.

## Receipts

Drop a receipt on the expense, or choose a file: a photo (JPG or PNG) or a
PDF. An expense can have more than one receipt.

## Categories

Under **Categories** on the Expenses page you add, rename and order your
categories. The order decides how they appear in drop-down lists and on the
year overview.

## Searching and the year overview

At the top of the list you search by supplier or description, and filter by
year and category.

**Reports → Year overview** shows your revenue, your costs and the difference
for each year, also per quarter. You can download a CSV there too, or an
export with everything in it. See [Exporting](exporteren.en.md).
