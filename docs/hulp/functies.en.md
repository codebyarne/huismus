# What Huismus can do

Everything Huismus does today on one page, and what's still coming. Huismus
is free and open source. Your records stay on your own computer or your own
server.

## Invoices and quotes

- **Invoices** with your logo, as PDF. An invoice gets a number only when you
  issue it, and the numbers run on without gaps. See
  [Invoices](facturen.en.md).
- **Payments**, also in parts. The overview shows what's outstanding and
  what's overdue.
- **Credit notes** to correct an issued invoice.
- **Quotes** that you send and turn into an invoice with one click. See
  [Quotes](offertes.en.md).
- **Clients**, each with their own payment terms if you want.
- **Mail** from Huismus, through your own Gmail or your own domain's mail
  server. See [Mail](mailen.en.md).

## Expenses and receipts

- **Expenses** with the receipt attached, in categories. See
  [Expenses](uitgaven.en.md).
- **A receipts folder**: on the road, put a photo in a folder that syncs, and
  Huismus picks it up. See [Receipts on the road](bonnetjes.en.md).
- **Reading receipts**: an AI service you choose fills in the expense for
  you. You check it.

## KOR and VAT

- **With the KOR**: no VAT on your invoices, your own KOR text below them, and
  on the overview how far you are from your KOR limit.
- **With VAT**: 0, 9 or 21% or reverse-charged per line, and the VAT on each
  receipt as input VAT.
- **VAT return**: per quarter, month or year the amounts per box, ready to
  copy. Huismus doesn't send anything itself. See
  [KOR and VAT](kor-en-btw.en.md).

## Overview and your accountant

- **Year overview**: per year your revenue, your costs and what's left, also
  per quarter, and as CSV.
- **Export**: one ZIP with everything as CSV, the PDFs and the receipts. It
  opens without Huismus too. See [Exporting](exporteren.en.md) and
  [For your accountant](boekhouder.en.md).

## Switching

- **From Moneybird** you bring your clients, invoices, quotes and expenses
  over one to one, with the PDFs and receipts. First a trial run, checked to
  the cent. Your invoice numbers carry on. See
  [Moving from Moneybird](moneybird.en.md).

## Your data

- **A backup every day**, to a folder, Dropbox or WebDAV. See
  [Backups](back-ups.en.md).
- **No account and no Huismus server.** Your data only goes somewhere if you
  turn that on.
- **An AI assistant** such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini can work with your
  records, if you connect it. See [AI assistants](ai-assistenten.en.md).

## Where it runs

- **As an app** on your Mac or Windows PC. It updates itself. See
  [The desktop app](desktop-app.en.md).
- **On your own server** with Docker, such as a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or a VPS.
  See [Huismus in Docker](docker.en.md).
- **On your phone**, when Huismus runs on a server. See
  [On your phone](telefoon.en.md).
- **In Dutch and English**, the app and your invoices.

## What's coming

This is on the list. The order may still change, and there's no date.

- Reading your bank statement, so you see which invoice is paid
- Mailing a payment reminder for an overdue invoice
- Tracking hours, and invoicing them
- Recurring invoices, such as the same one every month
- A profit and loss statement per year
- Tracking mileage
- Investments and depreciation
- An XAF audit file for your accountant
- Importing from Rompslomp and other packages
- Switching in the middle of the year
- Attaching a receipt from the receipts folder to an existing expense
- Backups to Google Drive, OneDrive and Proton Drive

## Maybe later

This can't be done with just your own computer. It needs a service on the
internet, so it only comes when that can be safe and simple.

- A connection with your bank
- Sending your VAT return from Huismus
- A payment link on the invoice
- An invoice or quote your client views or accepts online
- E-invoices through Peppol
- An app for your phone without your own server

## On purpose not

- Salaries and payroll
- A web shop
- One Huismus for several businesses
- Tax advice: Huismus adds up what you enter
