# Your data

Your records stay on your own computer or your own server. Huismus has no
account and no server of its own. It doesn't track who uses it or what you
do. Something only leaves your computer when you turn it on. Below is
everything that can be.

## When you turn it on

- **Email:** an invoice, quote or credit note you email goes to your client
  through your own Gmail or mail server. See [Mail](mailen.en.md).
- **Backups:** link Dropbox or WebDAV and a backup of your records goes there
  every day. See [Backups](back-ups.en.md).
- **Receipts folder:** with Dropbox or WebDAV, Huismus picks up new receipts
  from there. See [Receipts on the road](bonnetjes.en.md).
- **Reading receipts:** choose an AI service and each new receipt goes to that
  service, which fills in the expense for you.
- **AI assistant:** link ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and what the assistant
  reads goes to the company behind it. See
  [AI assistants](ai-assistenten.en.md).
- **How do you use Huismus?:** only when you press Send does what you filled
  in go to the app's maker. You see everything that goes first.

## What Huismus does by itself

- **Looking for a new version:** the desktop app and Docker ask GitHub every
  day whether there's a new version. Only the name and version of Huismus go
  along, nothing from your records. Turn it off in **Settings → Updates**.
  The Microsoft Store version doesn't look by itself: the Store does that.

## What Huismus never does

- Keep statistics, track you or show ads.
- Send your records to the app's maker.
