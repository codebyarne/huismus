# For your accountant

Does an accountant work with you, for example on your income tax return?
Then you give your accountant files from Huismus. Huismus has one user, so
your accountant doesn't log in, but works with what you download.

## What you hand over

- **The export**: one ZIP with your invoices, credit notes, quotes, clients
  and expenses as CSV, with every PDF and every receipt included. Open
  **Settings → Export**, choose the year and click **Download ZIP**.
  See [Exporting](exporteren.en.md).
- **The year overview**: your revenue, your costs and what's left, also per
  quarter. Open **Reports → Year overview**, choose the year and click
  **Download CSV**.
- **The VAT return**, if you charge VAT: the amounts per box. Open
  **Reports → VAT return**, choose the period and click **Download CSV**.
  See [KOR and VAT](kor-en-btw.en.md#vat-return).

The PDFs in the export are the same ones your client received. Each expense
has its receipt, named after the expense's number.

## What isn't there yet

Huismus doesn't make an XAF audit file yet, the file many accounting
programs read. So your accountant works with the CSVs and the PDFs. For what
else is coming, see [What Huismus can do](functies.en.md#whats-coming).
