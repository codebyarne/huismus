# Moving from Moneybird

Are your records in Moneybird? Then you bring your customers, invoices, quotes
and expenses over to Huismus one to one. The PDFs and receipts come along too.
Huismus does a trial run first and only saves anything when you choose to.

Only cancel Moneybird once everything is in Huismus. Your old years are then
on your own computer, with no subscription. What else to look out for is in
[Switching to Huismus](overstappen.en.md).

## Getting your data out of Moneybird

1. Log in to Moneybird and open the administration you want to move.
2. In the settings, go to the **bookkeeping export** and download the
   **year export**.
3. Choose a financial year and export it. Do this for every year you want to
   bring along.
4. Download the zips from the bell at the top right in Moneybird.

No subscription anymore? Then use the **export** button at the top right in
Moneybird and choose **all data**. You get a zip per year then too.

Don't unzip them. Huismus reads them as they are.

## The trial run

In Huismus, open **Import**, or choose **Import from Moneybird** on the empty
overview. Choose all the zips at once, or drop them in the box, and click
**Start trial run**.

You then see, for each part, what the import would do: how many customers,
invoices, quotes and expenses are new, which ones Huismus updates and which
already exist. Nothing is saved yet.

Huismus works out every invoice again and compares the total with Moneybird's,
to the cent. If one differs, you see which and by how much. That can be a
rounding cent, for example. Check those in Moneybird.

Huismus also fills in your business details from your newest invoice. Check
them: when you import, they go into **Settings**.

## Importing

All good? Click **Import now**. You don't have to choose the files again.
Then everything is in Huismus, with the numbers as they were in Moneybird.

- **PDFs:** Huismus keeps the PDF from Moneybird with each invoice, as the
  original next to its own version.
- **Receipts:** these go with your expenses.
- **Your export:** Huismus keeps the zips themselves too, and they go along
  in your backups.

## Importing again

If you import the same export again, nothing changes: Huismus skips what
already exists. So you can always import a newer year later.

## Cancelling afterwards

Is everything in Huismus, and does it add up? Then you can cancel Moneybird.
Your old years, the PDFs and your receipts stay in Huismus and in your
backups, with no subscription. First go through the steps in
[Switching to Huismus](overstappen.en.md#how-to-switch), and make sure you
have a backup.
