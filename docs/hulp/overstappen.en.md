# Moving to Huismus

Are your books in another bookkeeping app or in Excel? Then you bring them
along to Huismus: your customers, invoices, quotes and expenses, with the PDFs
and receipts. Huismus does a trial run first and only saves something when you
choose to.

## How to move

1. **Get everything out of your old bookkeeping app.** Every year you want to
   bring along, with the PDFs and receipts. Where to find it is on the page of
   your app below.
2. **Do a trial run.** Huismus shows what it would take over, checks every
   invoice, to the cent, and says what doesn't add up. Nothing has been saved
   yet.
3. **Import.** Is the trial run right? Then Huismus puts everything in. Your
   invoice numbers stay as they were, and your next invoice counts on. Want
   a different next number? You set it under **Settings → Invoices and
   numbering**.
4. **Check it.** Check your business details under **Settings**, your IBAN
   above all. Put a few invoices next to the PDF from your old app.
   Compare your revenue per year under **Reports › Year overview** with the
   one in your old app.

## Per app

- **[Moneybird](moneybird.en.md):** Huismus reads your export as it is, with
  all years at once.
- **[Rompslomp](rompslomp.en.md), [Tellow](tellow.en.md),
  [e-Boekhouden.nl](e-boekhouden.en.md) and [Jortt](jortt.en.md):** you get
  your data out and have an AI assistant convert it to the Huismus template.
  The page of your app says where to find the export and what to ask the
  assistant.
- **[Excel or Numbers](excel.en.md):** the same, with your own sheets.
- **Another bookkeeping app:** you fill in the template yourself, or have an AI
  assistant do it. See [Importing from another bookkeeping app or
  Excel](importeren.en.md).

## What to look out for

- **Your IBAN.** Did your invoices carry an account of your old bookkeeping
  app? Then enter the IBAN you want to be paid on from now on under
  **Settings**. A customer can still pay an open invoice to the old account.
  Keep an eye on it until everything has come in.
- **Your VAT return.** Do you work without the small business scheme (KOR)?
  Take the amounts of a quarter from one app: from your old one, or from
  Huismus under **Reports › VAT return**. That way you count nothing twice
  and miss nothing. Huismus doesn't file the return: you copy the amounts
  yourself. See [Small business scheme (KOR) and VAT](kor-en-btw.en.md).
- **The moment.** Pick a quiet moment, for example right after your VAT
  return or around 1 January. Then a whole quarter or year is in one
  app. Only make invoices in Huismus once everything from your old app
  is in.
- **Your bookkeeper.** Give your bookkeeper the export from Huismus: one zip
  with everything, the PDFs and receipts too. See
  [Exporting](exporteren.en.md).

## What you have afterwards

Your old years are in Huismus and in your backups, without a subscription.
With [Exporting](exporteren.en.md) you always get them out again, as files
that open without Huismus too.

How long you keep your records is on the site of the
[Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/administratie_bewaren/)
(in Dutch).
