# Switching to Huismus

Coming from Moneybird? Put all your years into Huismus first, and only cancel
after that. Your old years are then on your own computer, with no
subscription. Huismus can't import from other packages yet; see Per package
below.

## Import first, then cancel

From Moneybird, Huismus takes over your customers, invoices, quotes and
expenses, with the PDFs and receipts. Your invoice numbers simply carry on.
After the import your old years are in Huismus and in your backups. With
[Exporting](exporteren.en.md) you can always get them out again, as files
that open without Huismus too.

What happens to your data in your old package once you cancel is something to
look up in that package's help. So put everything into Huismus first.

## How to switch

1. **Export everything.** Get every year you want to bring along out of your
   old package, with the PDFs and receipts.
2. **The trial run.** Huismus shows what it would take over and recalculates
   every invoice, to the cent. It doesn't save anything yet.
3. **Import.** Does the trial run look right? Then Huismus saves it all.
4. **Check.** Look over your business details under **Settings →
   Business**, your IBAN above all. Put a few invoices next to the PDF from
   your old package, and check that **Reports → Year overview** matches your
   old package.
5. **Cancel.** Is everything in, and have you set up and made a backup
   (**Settings → Backups**)? Then cancel your old package.

## Per package

### Moneybird

Huismus brings everything over from Moneybird. How to export and import is in
[Moving from Moneybird](moneybird.en.md). How to cancel is something to look
up in [Moneybird's](https://www.moneybird.nl) help.

### Rompslomp, e-Boekhouden.nl or Tellow

Huismus can't import from these yet. It's on the list. Want to switch anyway?
Export everything your package offers and keep those files on your own
computer and with your backups, before you cancel. In Huismus you then start
fresh.

How to export and cancel is something to look up in the help of
[Rompslomp](https://www.rompslomp.nl),
[e-Boekhouden.nl](https://www.e-boekhouden.nl) or
[Tellow](https://www.tellow.nl).

### Excel

Huismus can't import from Excel yet either. Working in Excel? Keep those files
with your backups, and start fresh in Huismus.

## What to look out for

- **Your IBAN.** Huismus takes your business details from your newest
  invoice, including the IBAN on it. Was that an account from your old
  package? Then put the IBAN you want to be paid into from now on under
  **Settings → Business**. A customer may still pay an open invoice into the
  old account. Keep an eye on it until everything is in.
- **Your VAT return.** Not using the KOR? Take a quarter's amounts from one
  package: your old one, or Huismus under **Reports → VAT return**. That way
  you count nothing twice and miss nothing. Huismus doesn't file the return:
  you copy the amounts over yourself. See [KOR and VAT](kor-en-btw.en.md).
- **The moment.** Pick a quiet moment, for example right after your VAT
  return or around 1 January. Then a whole quarter or year sits in one
  package. Coming from Moneybird and still making invoices there in the
  meantime? Import a new export: whatever is already in Huismus won't come in
  twice. Don't make invoices in Huismus yet in the meantime, or two invoices
  end up with the same number.
- **Your accountant.** Give your accountant the export from Huismus: one ZIP
  with everything, PDFs and receipts included. See
  [Exporting](exporteren.en.md).

## What Huismus keeps

After the import from Moneybird, Huismus keeps your old years, the Moneybird
PDF with every invoice, your receipts and your original export. All of it goes
into your [backups](back-ups.en.md). How long to keep your records is
explained by the
[Belastingdienst](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/administratie_bewaren/)
(in Dutch).
